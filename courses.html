About each Courses

CSC301 - Computer Centre Management - 3 units
  Management, organization, and operation of computer centres and their resources.
CSC302 - Systems Analysis - 3 units
 Study of how to examine existing systems, identify problems, and design improved systems
CSC313 - Computer Architecture and Organization - 4 units
 How computers are structured and how components such as the CPU, memory, and I/O work together
CSC321 - Computer Operating Systems I - 3 units
 Fundamentals of operating systems, including process, memory, file, and resource management.
CSC322 - Principles of Data Communication - 2 units
 How data is transmitted between computers and devices through communication networks
CSC331 - Programming Principles and Paradigms - 3 units
 Fundamental programming concepts and different approaches to writing and organizing programs
CSC334 - Systems Programming - 4 units
 Programming close to the operating system, including processes, memory, files, and system resources
CSC335 - Software Engineering - 4 units
 Principles and methods for systematically developing, testing, maintaining, and managing software
CSC351 - Formal Languages and Automata Theory - 3 units
 Mathematical models of computation, including finite automata, regular languages, grammars, and related concepts
CSC399 - Industrial Training II - 2 units
 Practical work experience where you apply computer science knowledge in a real-world work environment.
MAT352 - Probability Distributions and Elementary Limit Theories - 4 units
 Study of probability distributions and basic concepts involving limits and their applications
MAT353 - Introduction to Operations Research - 4 units
 Mathematical techniques for solving optimization and decision-making problems.
GES103 - Government, Society and The Economy - 2 units
 Introduction to the relationship between government, society, and economic activities.
GES301 - Introduction Entrepreneurial Skills - 2 units
 Basic entrepreneurial knowledge and skills for identifying opportunities and developing businesses.
CSC341 - Data Structures - 4 units
 Methods of organizing and storing data efficiently, including arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, and graphs
CSC381 - Computer Graphics - 3 units
 Principles and techniques for creating, manipulating, and displaying computer-generated images and graphics
